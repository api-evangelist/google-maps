Innobrix, a Dutch startup, won the Google Maps Platform Award for its groundbreaking 3D platform that brings real-world context to construction projects. By combining web-first, mobile-friendly technology with 3D Maps from Google Maps Platform, Innobrix empowers everyone, from architects to homebuyers, to visualize and configure homes in real environments. Their story shows how thoughtful design, user feedback, and technical innovation can transform even the most traditional industries.
