Last year, we launched Roads Management Insights (RMI) to empower public sector road managers with the deep network intelligence needed to optimize civic infrastructure. Today at Cloud Next, we’re taking that mission of safety, efficiency, and reliability a step further. As part of Google Earth AI, we’re introducing a suite of powerful new RMI capabilities for our existing users and opening the product to the private sector, connecting the agencies that maintain the roads with the commercial fleets that drive on them.
