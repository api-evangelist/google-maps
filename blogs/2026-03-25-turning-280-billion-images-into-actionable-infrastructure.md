Today, we’re excited to announce that the first dataset, Street View Insights, is now Generally Available. This powerful dataset taps into Google Street View’s vast repository of over 280 billion 360° and 3D images covering infrastructure across 110+ countries. It allows organizations—such as telecommunications providers, utilities, and public works agencies—to reduce costly, time-consuming field surveys and gain actionable infrastructure insights right from their desks.
