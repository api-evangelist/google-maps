Today we are excited to announce the General Availability of two Places UI Kit components: Place Search and Autocomplete, building on the launch of the Place Details component in June. Available through the Maps JavaScript API and the iOS and Android SDKs, these components enable you to create impactful front-end experiences with just a few lines of code, helping users explore, discover, and find places with ease.
