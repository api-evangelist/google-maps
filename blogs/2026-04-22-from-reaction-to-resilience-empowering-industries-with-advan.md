Today at Cloud Next we’re introducing new environment datasets, part of Google Earth AI, now available in Experimental in Google Maps Platform. These datasets complement our suite of Environment APIs and provide air quality, pollen and weather insights. This enables you to go beyond the API’s real-time data and unlock environmental insights through hyper-local, high-resolution historical data, seamlessly integrated with BigQuery.
