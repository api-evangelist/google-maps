Today, we are introducing detect change, an experimental tool in Google Earth on web that finds where and when change has occurred anywhere on Earth. Powered by Google's latest geospatial AI technology, the tool compares Earth observation data between two annual intervals, generating a 10-meter resolution heatmap layer and fast tracking your work. Detect change is available today on Google Earth web and Android for Standard plan users, with expanded capabilities available on professional plans.
